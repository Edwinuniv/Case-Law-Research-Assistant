# Plan after the meeting with Dr. Salloum (2026-11-13)

What he asked, our decisions, who builds what, and in which order. Recorded in SPEC section 11 (2026-11-13).
Every change keeps the project's rules: tests first, small commits on a branch, a rule in EVALUATION.md before any
measurement, and **the AI never invents a citation, never gives an opinion, and never answers outside the law**.

## What he asked
1. **Follow-up questions are required** (not only allowed). The model reads the whole chat to continue it.
2. **Visitors without an account**: only one question, and their chat is not saved. **Logged-in users** can send
   messages and keep their chat.
3. **Admins see every question** asked, to improve the site and the AI's answers.
4. **Admins see the list of users** with their details (sign-up, logins, last activity, plan, questions...).
5. **PDF exports** for admins: one user with their details, or the list of all users.
6. **Logs**: one log per user (everything that user does, shown in the user's details) and a separate admin log
   (everything the admins do).
7. **Free and paid accounts.**
8. **Smarter answers**: fuller first answers (not three sentences then the passages), follow-ups that reuse the
   passages already sent unless the new question needs new ones.
9. **Prompt caching and fewer tokens.**
10. **Demo questions saved in the database**: the suggested questions and their answers are stored, to show the jury
    without spending tokens or waiting.
11. **Still**: no invented citation, no opinion, nothing unrelated to the passages or to the law. A question that is
    not about the law gets a simple message: Verdicta is a case-law research assistant.

## Our decisions (2026-11-13)
- Visitors without an account: **1 question per browser session**; the question is **logged for the admins**
  (anonymous, as today), but the visitor gets **no saved chat**. Then "log in to continue".
- Plans: **free = 10 questions a day, paid = 100 a day**; an admin switches an account to paid (no real payment:
  a student project, said on the Terms page). The limits are settings, changeable.
- Prompt caching: Groq caches automatically when requests share the same beginning (gpt-oss-120b, cached input at half
  price, and cached tokens do not count toward the rate limits). So every request starts with what does not change
  (the rules, then the earlier turns of the chat) and ends with what is new.
